# MUSE ✦ — AI Creative Assistant for MasteringIt

> *"Your personal creative partner — always ready to brainstorm Facebook posts, course outlines, newsletters, and more."*

**Live demo:** [amesie.github.io/Muse-Chatbot](https://amesie.github.io/Muse-Chatbot/)

---

## What is MUSE?

MUSE is a branded AI creative assistant built for **MasteringIt**, a Cape Town-based tutoring and career services company. It was designed to solve a very specific problem: the mental exhaustion of starting from a blank page.

Rather than asking a business owner to wrangle a generic AI tool into sounding like her brand, MUSE comes pre-loaded with MasteringIt's full service catalogue, pricing, tone of voice, and brand personality — so every output is ready to use with minimal editing.

---

## The problem it solves

Through a structured design thinking process — including empathy interviews, user observation, and iterative prototyping — a key insight emerged: content creation was more draining than any administrative task, not because of time, but because of the cognitive cost of starting from nothing.

MUSE was built to eliminate the blank page entirely.

---

## Features

- 💬 **On-demand chat interface** — activate it when you need it, not on a schedule
- ⚡ **Quick prompt buttons** — one-click starters for common content types (weekly content plan, study skills post, personal story post, career test post, monthly newsletter, CV course outline)
- 🎨 **Content generation** — social media captions, post templates, course outlines, newsletters
- 🧠 **Brand-aware** — hard-coded with MasteringIt's tone, services, rates, and course offerings so outputs never sound generic or robotic
- 🔐 **Passkey protected** — simple access control to keep it private to the business owner
- 🔄 **New Session button** — clear the chat and start fresh at any time

---

## Known limitations

- **20 requests/day** on the free Gemini tier — may be limiting during an active content session
- Generated visual templates are starting points and still require editing in Canva before posting
- No persistent memory — conversations do not save between sessions
- Templates cannot be directly downloaded and must be screenshotted

MUSE was developed as part of a university Design Thinking module (2025), following a full double-diamond process including stakeholder interviews, problem definition, ideation, prototyping, and user testing with the actual SMME owner.
